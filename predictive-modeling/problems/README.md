Each statement follows the same shape: the question, what already exists, where it lives, a
first move, what you will learn, **the catch**, and the open questions nobody has answered yet.

The catch section is the most important part of each one. Every dataset here was collected by
someone solving a different problem, and every one of these problems has something wrong with
it. Knowing what, before you start, is most of the job.
