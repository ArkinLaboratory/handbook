# Onboarding for UC Berkeley students, volunteers and affiliates

The main [onboarding checklist](README.md) assumes an LBNL appointment. Many group members —
undergraduates, UC Berkeley volunteers, masters students, visiting researchers — do not have
one. This page is the path for them.

## What you can get without any LBNL appointment

| Resource | Needs LBNL? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Group servers** (`niya`, `thar`) | **No** | Keith creates a local account. This is the normal path for students. |
| **Savio** (UC Berkeley cluster) | **No** | CalNet ID only. Join project `ac_arkinlab`. |
| **GitHub** `ArkinLaboratory` org | No | Ask Adam for an invite. |
| **Lab Slack** | No | Ask Adam or Gwyneth. |
| **KBase account** | No | Sign up with your ORCID. |
| **CBORG** (`cborg.lbl.gov`) | **Yes** | Free for `@lbl.gov` accounts. Without one, you cannot complete this step — say so rather than silently skipping it. |
| **Lawrencium** | **Yes** | Needs LBNL identity, plus a standing per-user monthly fee. |
| **LBL badge / on-site access** | **Yes** | Requires an affiliate appointment. |
| **BER Lakehouse** | Partly | KBase account gets you in; the LLM-key step needs CBORG, hence an LBL account. |

**Start with the group server and Savio.** Between them they cover everything most people need
for the first month, and neither requires LBNL paperwork.

## If you do need LBNL access

Handled by **Gwyneth Terry** (`gaterry@lbl.gov`). Expect this to take weeks, not days.

- An **affiliate appointment** requires a *sponsoring institution*. UC Berkeley affiliate status
  does not itself qualify — if you are a UCB volunteer rather than a registered student, you may
  need to be enrolled somewhere (a community college course is sufficient) for LBNL to sponsor
  you. This has caught people out; ask Gwyneth early.
- **A badge cannot be issued before your appointment's official start date.** If HR gives you a
  start date, that is the earliest you can book a badge appointment.
- **Required training** (GERT and others) depends on your appointment type.
- **If you are not a US person**, the Foreign Visits and Assignments process applies and
  typically takes **about 60 days**. Start it as early as possible, and plan your first semester
  around the group servers and Savio.

## Work authorization

If you are on a student visa, check your hours limit and whether your appointment counts as
on-campus or off-campus work **with the Berkeley International Office** — not with the lab. Get
the answer in writing before you start. Nobody here can give you an authoritative answer on
this, and assuming is expensive.
